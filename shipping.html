Shipping Policy

Everything you need to know about book delivery, shipping timelines and order tracking at MindShift.

Last updated: October 2026

On this page

Introduction Delivery Coverage Order Processing Delivery Timelines Shipping Charges Order Tracking Delivery Delays Delivery Address Damaged Packages Contact Us

01. Introduction

Welcome to MindShift! This Shipping Policy explains how book orders may be processed and delivered when our shipping service is available.

Please review this policy before placing an order on a live shopping website.

02. Delivery Coverage

Delivery availability may depend on your location and the courier services available in your area.

For a live service, eligible delivery locations will be confirmed during checkout or before an order is accepted.

03. Order Processing

Once a customer places an order through an active ordering system, the order details may be reviewed before dispatch.

  • Order details should be accurate.
  • Payment confirmation may be required where applicable.
  • Dispatch updates should be shared when available.

Processing times depend on stock availability and the actual operations of the seller.

04. Delivery Timelines

Delivery timelines depend on the destination, courier service, stock availability and other operational factors.

Any estimated delivery date provided on a live website is an estimate and may change due to circumstances beyond the seller's control.

MindShift's current demo does not provide a confirmed real-world delivery timeline.

05. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, should be displayed before a customer confirms a real order.

Charges may depend on delivery location, order value, package details and the shipping options offered.

Any free-shipping offer should clearly state its eligibility conditions.

06. Order Tracking

If shipment tracking is available, customers may receive a tracking number or delivery updates after dispatch.

Tracking information depends on the courier service and may take time to appear after the package is shipped.

07. Delivery Delays

Delivery may be delayed because of weather conditions, courier disruptions, public holidays, incorrect address details or other unexpected circumstances.

Where a live shipping service is available, customers should be informed of significant delays when reasonably possible.

08. Delivery Address

Customers should provide a complete and accurate delivery address and contact details when placing an order.

If an address needs to be changed after an order is placed, customers should contact support as soon as possible. Changes may not be possible after dispatch.

09. Damaged or Missing Packages

If a delivered package appears damaged or an item is missing, customers should contact the seller promptly and provide the order details and relevant photographs, where available.

Any replacement, return or refund will be handled according to the applicable Return & Refund Policy and consumer rights.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about shipping or delivery, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
